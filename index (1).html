Data science check: simulated cohort evaluation

Generates 600 synthetic patients (half stable, half deteriorating) with the same simulator, then compares a plain threshold alert (score 7 or more) with the forecast early warning. Synthetic data only: it demonstrates the method, not clinical accuracy.

    All patients, vitals and alerts here are simulated for the demo. Scores follow the NEWS2 bands for pulse, systolic BP, SpO₂ (scale 1), temperature and respiratory rate. Consciousness and supplemental oxygen are not collected, activity level is tracked but not scored, and the glucose bands are demo thresholds set by the team that need clinical validation. The trend summary is generated from rules in this prototype. This is not medical advice.
